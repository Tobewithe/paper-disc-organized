## Method contract

Frozen official COCO YOLO26m-seg, Ultralytics 8.4.100, unfused FP32 eval, immutable weights SHA256 `16b636f04e8fb6a325b3370f22dc5e5535ff473e384f4d041fd28d788f6ee9f5`. Every YOLO parameter, BN affine/buffer and feature/prototype/coefficient branch remains frozen. Train only the new TriFlow module. Do not start from an ACD-finetuned checkpoint.

Use real native P3 features, shared 32-channel prototype P, one2one c0 and coefficient-head hidden query. Preserve native top-k raw-index/class-row identity, bbox, score, class and order. Inputs use 640 square LetterBox, scaleup=false, batch1, FP32, TF32=false. No training augmentation; full FP32 frozen image features are cached once and hashed.

Conflict tokenizer selects 64 positions from boundary uncertainty, predicted instance competition and prototype leverage; it does not select positions with GT. Three ownership queries (Self/Neighbor/Background) interact with local F/P/spatial gradients/current and neighbor logits and the native hidden query. The model predicts continuous phi_N, phi_B and nonzero-floor stiffness; it must not directly predict delta_c or substitute ownership cross-entropy for potential supervision.

GT Self is the target original COCO annotation raster. Neighbor is union of other noncrowd instance masks excluding Self. Self wins overlaps. Crowd pixels outside Self are ignored, never known Background. Background is outside all ordinary instances and crowd. Compute relative potentials from untruncated EDT differences, then clip/normalize by 16 prototype-grid pixels. Empty GT regions are explicitly invalid; absent predicted neighbor contexts disable Neighbor constraints and corresponding potential supervision. Background remains explicitly distinct from Neighbor. Record missing-neighbor cases; this first implementation does not infer a competing predicted logit for an undetected neighbor.

Important correction to the proposal: z>0 is foreground, therefore outward normal is n_out=-grad(z)/||grad(z)||. Positive delta_s means expansion and the band-point shape target is p_x^T delta_c = delta_s*||grad(z)|| - z(x). Use Self-boundary intersections of min(valid phi_N, phi_B), with signed interpolation preserving gradients. A root of one potential is a Self boundary only when the other valid potential is nonnegative. No-root/flat-gradient rows are invalid, not fabricated displacements.

Region anchors are one-sided Self/Neighbor/BG margin inequalities; currently satisfied anchors exert zero force. A fixed three-pass differentiable active-hinge compiler solves a normalized weighted 32x32 SPD ridge system, re-evaluating anchor violations after each candidate update. It is a finite-pass approximation and does not certify global KKT optimality or satisfaction of every constraint after trust clipping. Normalize ROI Gram and row terms by effective counts; use explicit epsilon, condition/residual diagnostics and conservative logit/coefficient trust bounds. No learned 32D update head. Token/root-segment selection may be discrete, but field values -> interpolation -> constraints/stiffness -> solve -> refined coefficient -> final mask must remain differentiable.

Potential SmoothL1, direction cosine, local normal ordering and final mask task loss are all present. Stiffness has a floor and calibration supervision. Exact defaults and parameter counts are archived in each Run's source/config receipt; no setting is tuned on full val. Mathematical and actual frozen-forward diagnostics, including finite nonzero task-only gradients through Compiler to potential/interaction parameters, must pass before formal training. A fixture warm-up is diagnostic only and cannot be silently counted as formal training.
