## Evidence extraction

- Problem: highly overlapping objects make object contours and occlusion boundaries ambiguous.
- Mechanism: BCNet represents occluder and occludee as two layers; convolutional, graph-convolutional and query variants explicitly model their interaction during mask regression.
- Training/inference location: relation is modeled inside the network and mask regression, not as a post-hoc NMS rule.
- Data/metrics: COCO, KINS, COCOA, YTVIS, OVIS and BDD100K MOTS; abstract reports consistent improvements, without importing unverified numeric values.
- Limitation for this project: evidence targets visible overlap/occlusion; it does not establish a solution for low-IoU complementary fragments where full and local candidates co-survive.
- Relation to evidence: supports testing explicit instance relations as a hypothesis; does not prove causal applicability to `SPLIT_TYPE`.
