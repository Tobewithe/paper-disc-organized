# Instance Segmentation of Visible and Occluded Regions for Finding and Picking Target from a Pile of Objects

- Authors: Kentaro Wada; Shingo Kitagawa; Kei Okada; Masayuki Inaba
- Year / venue: 2018; IROS
- Identifier: arXiv:2001.07475
- Official source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.07475
- Sources: DeepXiv, arXiv
- Verification: arXiv metadata and IROS record in DeepXiv.

## Evidence extraction

- Problem: instance segmentation and picking-order planning under heavy clutter and occlusion.
- Mechanism: a relook architecture explicitly learns inter-instance relationships and predicts visible and occluded regions.
- Training/inference location: relation-aware relook stage inside the segmentation system.
- Limitation: visible/occluded mask semantics and pile-picking setting differ from still-image low-IoU fragment competition.
- Relation: precedent for relation-aware refinement; transfer to `SPLIT_TYPE` is unverified.
