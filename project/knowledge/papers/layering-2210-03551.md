# Instance Segmentation of Dense and Overlapping Objects via Layering

- Authors: Long Chen; Yuli Wu; Dorit Merhof
- Year / venue: 2022; BMVC
- Identifier: arXiv:2210.03551
- Official source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03551
- Sources: arXiv, Semantic Scholar, DeepXiv
- Verification: arXiv metadata plus S2 venue record.

## Evidence extraction

- Problem: segmenting dense, overlapping objects.
- Mechanism: distribute crowded objects into layers by grouping spatially separated instances in the same layer; layer assignment supplies a structured decomposition.
- Training/inference location: structured instance grouping before/within segmentation inference; exact implementation details require paper deep read.
- Data/metrics: BMVC experiments; no numeric result copied without full-text verification.
- Limitation: designed for dense overlap and layering, not explicitly for low-IoU fragment competition or YOLO26 candidate scores.
- Relation: a candidate mechanism for structured competition, but transfer to pig masks remains an open question.
