## Evidence extraction

- Mechanism: OCFusion learns binary instance occlusion relations instead of sorting instances only by confidence.
- Limitation: relation is explicitly occlusion-oriented; no direct evidence for complementary low-IoU fragments.
- Relation: prior art for replacing confidence-only selection with structured relations; applicability to `SPLIT_TYPE` remains a testable option.
