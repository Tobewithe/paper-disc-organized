\section*{Known data and implementation sources}
The study uses COCO val2017 annotations and images (5{,}000 images, 36{,}335 ordinary GT), the
COCO-pretrained Ultralytics YOLO26m-seg weights, and Ultralytics 8.4.100. All readouts are
diagnostics on the frozen checkpoint; no weights are trained. Every number in the manuscript
traces to a retained run record in the project archive (protocols frozen before execution,
per-candidate/per-GT records, independent verification runs, source snapshots with hashes,
transfer manifests); the claim-to-record map is maintained as the study evidence map. Dataset
images and segmenter weights are not bundled.
